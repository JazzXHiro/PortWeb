Overview

Jaded is a 2D monster-catching game where you explore a hand-built world, discover creatures with their own abilities, and capture, train, and battle your way toward becoming the ultimate monster master.

The whole thing runs on a custom engine loop written on top of Pygame — collision, movement, animation timing and a turn-based battle system were all built from scratch rather than pulled from a framework. That constraint was the point: it forced every system to be understood end to end.

Art, creature design, systems and code are all solo work, which kept the vision tight and the iteration loop fast.

Core Features

Custom Physics

Capture, train and battle with a strategy layer — type matchups, status effects and switching.

Explorable World

Distinct biomes — forest, caves and more — each seeded with their own creatures to find.

Inventory & Progression

Items, capture tools and a growing roster that persists as you train your team up.

Built From Scratch
